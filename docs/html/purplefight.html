The Purple Fight —credits scenes

description

For The Purple Fight, a stop-motion short film about feminism and women’s suffrage, I was responsible for designing all the characters and backgrounds. I carefully considered how the characters would articulate and move, as well as the scaling of elements depending on their position in the scene—foreground or background. Additionally, I created a 2D animation of the protagonist for the film’s end credits, maintaining visual consistency with the story.
