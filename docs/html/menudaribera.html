Menuda Ribera —social media illustrations

description

A series of social media illustrations designed to visually communicate the steps of a community-driven plantation project. The illustrations guided participants through the process of adopting, caring for, and planting their trees during a collective celebration day. The visuals were crafted to be clear, engaging, and aligned with the campaign’s environmental mission.
