Por la III República —posters

description

This series of posters was designed for the event 'Por la III República', capturing the essence of vintage political prints. The design utilizes halftones, a limited color palette, and textures that mimic the effect of screen printing ink, evoking the aesthetic of historical protest posters. The project includes a general poster displaying the full event schedule, alongside individual designs highlighting key talks and activities. Each piece maintains a cohesive visual identity while emphasizing the significance of each discussion within the event.
