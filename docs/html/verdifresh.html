VerdiFresh —style guide

description

As part of a team project, I was responsible for the layout of the brand’s style guide. Using the provided illustrations, graphic assets, typography, and color palette, I structured and designed the guide to clearly present all its sections, ensuring a cohesive and visually engaging document. Additionally, I focused on effectively showcasing the packaging characteristics within the guide. While I didn’t create the illustrations or design the packaging itself, my role was to organize and refine the brand’s visual guidelines into a polished and functional resource.
