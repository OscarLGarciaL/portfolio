I Tu, Sols Tu —music video

description

This 2D animation project brings to life La Jabalina, a historical figure from my hometown, through a collage of old photographs that connect with the town’s past. The animation is part of a music video for the song 'I tu, sols tu' by El Diluvi, a feminist anthem, featuring actresses signing in Spanish Sign Language (LSE). The project aims to raise awareness of the discrimination faced by deaf women and to empower them through sign language, a key element of Deaf culture and identity.
