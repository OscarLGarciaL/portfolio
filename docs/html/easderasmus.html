EASD —Erasmus Booklet

description

This Erasmus booklet was designed around the concept of discovery, providing students with clear and structured information about exchange program courses. To keep production costs low, it is printed entirely in black, with different specialties distinguished by the color of the paper. A key feature of the design is its modularity—held together by an elastic band, the booklet can be easily taken apart to update or replace pages each year, ensuring the information remains current without the need for a full reprint.
