Biblioteques de Sagunt

description

I redesigned the 'Biblioteques de Sagunt' logo to create a more cohesive and versatile visual identity. The concept for the new version combines the initials 'B' and 'S' with the shape of stacked books, reinforcing the essence of a library. Beyond updating the main logo, I saw the need to distinguish the two main libraries in town with their own variations. Each version is inspired by the architectural features of their entrances, simplified into a modern and recognizable design. To further differentiate them, I assigned a unique color to each, ensuring individuality while maintaining a unified brand identity.
