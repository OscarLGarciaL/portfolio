Montán —redesign

description

Redesign of Montán's brand identity, rooted in an in-depth study of its heraldry, history, and cultural heritage. Each element and color in the coat of arms was carefully analyzed to preserve its symbolic value while modernizing its form. Through research, I discovered that the town was originally established due to its water sources, making blue the most historically significant color. This insight led to its prominence in the new identity. The updated logo was constructed using a grid system to ensure equilibrium, proportionality, and consistency, providing a contemporary yet respectful evolution of the town's emblem.
