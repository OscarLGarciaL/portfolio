Fiesta Roja —poster

description

For this project, I designed a poster and a pamphlet for "Fiesta Roja", an event deeply rooted in social and political activism. The visual identity reflects its themes through bold iconography inspired by the movement’s values, incorporating raised fists as a symbol of unity and resistance. Additionally, elements of the town’s most iconic architecture were integrated into the composition, reinforcing a sense of historical pride and local identity.
