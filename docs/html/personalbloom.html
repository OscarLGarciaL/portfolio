Personal Bloom —logo reveal for SYFY

description

SYFY launched a seasonal initiative inviting artists, animators, designers, and directors to creatively reimagine their logo in a series of unique and visually striking TV spots. For this project, the goal was to craft an animation that would subvert audience expectations, bringing the SYFY logo to life in an unexpected way. The assigned theme was ‘subversive springtime’, challenging us to introduce a stereotypical vision of spring and then disrupt it with an unpredictable twist.
